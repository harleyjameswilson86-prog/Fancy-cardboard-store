Pickup Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Pickup Location

You can pick up your order from our warehouse location on the Gold Coast. We will email you when your order is ready for pickup with the warehouse address.

Pickup Hours

Our pickup hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Please contact us if you need to arrange a different time.

Order Processing

Orders are typically ready for pickup within 1-2 business days. You will receive an email notification when your order is ready.

What to Bring

Please bring a valid ID and your order confirmation email when picking up your order.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Pickup Policy, please contact us at hello@fancycardboardstore.com
